Approved Recommendations:
By AAP-42, it was announced that the following ITU-T Recommendations were approved, in accordance with the
procedures outlined in Recommendation ITU-T A.8:
–       ITU-T Y.2258 (08/2026): Service model of intelligent device management based on augmented reality in future
        network
–       ITU-T Y.2326 (08/2026): Network attachment control framework in Next Generation Network evolution control
        plane
–       ITU-T Y.2354 (08/2026): Requirements of Next Generation Network evolution (NGNe) to support multi-
        connection for network and cloud interworking
–       ITU-T Y.2362 (08/2026): Application-driven network configuration requirements for multi-user telerobotics-based
        solutions for developing countries
–       ITU-T Y.2505 (08/2026): Requirements and architecture of Computing Power Network control layer for network
        resource in Next Generation Network evolution
–       ITU-T Y.3231 (08/2026): Fixed, mobile and satellite convergence - Network enhancements of supporting
        broadcast and multicast for IMT-2020 networks and beyond
–       ITU-T Y.3232 (08/2026): Fixed, mobile and satellite convergence - Store and forward for IMT-2020 networks and
        beyond
–       ITU-T Y.3262 (08/2026): Overview of trust provisioning for networks and services using AI Technologies
–       ITU-T Y.3263 (08/2026): Framework of a trustworthy metaverse infrastructure
–       ITU-T Y.3330 (08/2026): Requirements and framework for traffic flow coordination of multi-modality
        communication in IMT-2020 networks and beyond
–       ITU-T Y.3331 (08/2026): Functional enhancement to support user plane computing in IMT-2020 networks and
        beyond
–       ITU-T Y.3332 (08/2026): Framework of joint development and operation for IMT-2020 networks and beyond
–       ITU-T Y.3333 (08/2026): Data models for intent-based network management and orchestration
–       ITU-T Y.3334 (08/2026): Information-centric networking in networks beyond IMT-2020 - Framework and
        capabilities of elastic control plane
–       ITU-T Y.3335 (08/2026): Framework of low-latency and energy-efficient networks
–       ITU-T Y.3336 (08/2026): Future networks including IMT-2020 - Requirements and framework of distributed data
        plane
–       ITU-T Y.3561 (08/2026): Cloud Computing - Principles of Cloud Native Application Development
–       ITU-T Y.3562 (08/2026): Cloud computing - Framework and functional requirements of microservice management
–       ITU-T Y.3609 (08/2026): Big data - Functional requirements for data supplier
–       ITU-T Y.3663 (08/2026): Big data driven networking - Architecture and mechanism of knowledge construction
–       ITU-T Y.3836 (08/2026): QoS assurance requirements for quantum key distribution networks enabled by software
        defined networking control
–       ITU-T Y.3837 (08/2026): Requirements of quality of service assurance for quantum key distribution network
        resilience

                                   National Numbering Plan
                    (According to Recommendation ITU-T E.129 (01/2013))
                                              See URL: www.itu.int/itu-t/nnp
Administrations are requested to notify ITU about their national numbering plan changes, or to give an explanation on
their webpage concerning the national numbering plan as well as their contact points, so that the information, which
will be made available freely to all administrations/ROAs and service providers, can be posted on the ITU-T website.
For their numbering website, or when sending their information to ITU/TSB (e-mail: tsbtson@itu.int), administrations
are kindly requested to use the format as explained in Recommendation ITU-T E.129. They are reminded that they will
be responsible for the timely update of this information.
